Mostly behind the scenes. Somewhere behind [BetterSolano.org](https://bettersolano.org): the commits land, the portal stays up, and that's usually all anyone sees.

## How I can help

- **Collaborations** on BetterSolano.org, from code and design to data and content.
- **New features and ideas** for the portal: what's missing, what could work better.
- **Bugs and wrong data** on BetterSolano.org, including broken pages, outdated information, and accessibility problems.
- **Starting your own portal** from the [Better Solano Starter](https://github.com/BetterSolano/bettersolano) template, if you're building a Better LGU portal for another town.
- **Any BetterGov.ph concern** where I can help or point you to the right people.

## Reaching out

Just ping me on [Discord](https://discord.gg/9rQZDQUXhW). That's where I'm most likely to see it first. For longer or more formal messages, like partnership proposals or anything with attachments, email **hello@ramonloganjr.com**.

For a bug, a link to the page and a screenshot or the steps to reproduce it help a lot. You can also open an issue on the [BetterSolano repository](https://github.com/BetterSolano/bettersolano/issues) so it's tracked. I do this as a volunteer, so replies come when I can, not on a fixed schedule.

## Not a fit for

- **Partisan or campaign work.** BetterSolano.org serves every resident, whoever is in office, so I keep it and myself non-partisan. I won't build for, endorse, or promote any candidate, party, or campaign.
- **Municipal services and complaints.** I'm a volunteer, not an employee of the Municipality of Solano or any LGU, so I can't process requests, permits, or documents, or act on complaints. For guides on local services, offices, and hotlines, check [BetterSolano.org](https://bettersolano.org); for an actual request or complaint, contact the Municipal Hall directly.
