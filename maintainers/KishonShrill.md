## Not a fit for

- **Partisan or campaign work**: I keep my civic tech non-partisan, so I won’t build for, endorse, or promote any candidate or party.
- **City services and complaints**: I’m a volunteer, not part of the Iligan City's local government unit , so I can’t process requests or act on complaints. For guides on city services, offices, and hotlines, check the official [Iligan City portal](https://iligan.gov.ph/); to file a permit, document request, or complaint, contact City Hall directly.
